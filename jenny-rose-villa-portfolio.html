Case Study 01

How I organized purchase order tracking and ensured accurate invoice management

The Problem

The client's purchase orders were listed in a master sheet with restricted editing access. Invoice details from suppliers were often missing or scattered, and order confirmation numbers didn't always match email records — making it impossible to track which orders had arrived or collect invoices for monthly accounting.

The Solution

I created a separate tracking sheet that pulled all key purchase order details from emails and the master sheet. I added a column to check delivery address, invoice status, and order number after each confirmation. A daily review routine was established: verify delivery status, then log the tracking number once confirmed.

✦ Result

Within one month: 100% on-time invoice collection. The accountant closed monthly books faster, reducing delays in financial reporting across the board.

Case Study 02

Successfully ungated a restricted Amazon brand after repeated rejections

The Problem

A brand being sourced on Amazon suddenly became restricted. Multiple attempts to ungate it over several months were rejected, blocking access to a profitable product line with no easy alternative available.

The Solution

I reviewed every rejection carefully to understand why it was denied. Each new application was improved by fixing required documents, organizing invoices properly, and matching Amazon's requirements precisely. A simple tracking sheet monitored each attempt, capturing lessons from previous mistakes to make each submission stronger.

✦ Result

After 6+ months of persistent attempts, the brand was successfully ungated — reopening a profitable sourcing channel that had been blocked entirely.
